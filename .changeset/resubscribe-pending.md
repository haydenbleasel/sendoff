Pressing **Resubscribe** for a signup that isn't confirmed yet puts the person back on the list, instead of saying emails to the address bounced or were marked as spam. Unsubscribing the signup and then resubscribing already did this, and the link comes from an email sent to that inbox, as a confirmation link does. Anyone else who can't be resubscribed, including someone whose address bounced while the page was loading, still sees **Can't resubscribe**.
