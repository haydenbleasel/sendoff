Pressing **Resubscribe** for a signup that isn't confirmed yet confirms it and puts the person back on the list, instead of saying emails to the address bounced or were marked as spam. Unsubscribing the signup and then resubscribing already did this, and the link comes from an email sent to that inbox, as a confirmation link does. Pressing **Resubscribe** twice no longer tells someone the first press put back on the list that their address bounced. Only bounced and complained addresses see **Can't resubscribe**.
